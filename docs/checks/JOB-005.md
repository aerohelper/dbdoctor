ID: JOB-005

Name:
Missing failure notifications

Severity:
WARNING

Why:
Without an email or webhook notification configured for failure, a failing job can go
unnoticed until someone happens to check the job list.

Detection:
No email notification and no webhook notification configured for the "on failure" event.

Recommendation:
Configure a failure notification so job failures don't go unnoticed.

Can auto-fix:
No
