ID: JOB-003

Name:
Job runtime is excessive

Severity:
WARNING

Why:
A job whose most recent run took an unusually long time may be regressing, stuck on a
retry loop, or processing more data than intended.

Detection:
`lastRunDurationMinutes > 180` (default threshold, configurable). Jobs whose run history
couldn't be fetched are skipped rather than flagged.

Recommendation:
Investigate why the job is taking this long, and consider a lower configured timeout.

Can auto-fix:
No
